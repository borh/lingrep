# Third-party notices for the lingrep-web browser assets

`editor/vendor/codemirror.js` is an esbuild bundle of the packages below, at
the versions in `lingrep-web/editor/package-lock.json`. `editor/vendor/web-tree-sitter.js`
and `editor/vendor/web-tree-sitter.wasm` are web-tree-sitter 0.26.11.
`vendor/htmx.min.js` and `vendor/htmx-ext-sse.min.js` are htmx 2.0.8 and
htmx-ext-sse 2.2.4.
